### About Me

I am a PhD Candidate and Research Associate in Human-Computer Interaction at the Institute of Media Informatics, Ulm University, Germany.

My research focuses on adaptive interactive systems that learn from users and respond to their goals, abilities, and contexts. I combine empirical HCI, computational methods, and prototyping to design and evaluate personalized interfaces for automated mobility, XR, and safety-critical human-AI interaction.

I also build simulation-based research tools, including immersive driving environments and human-in-the-loop optimization pipelines, to study personalization, trust, and usability in controlled but realistic interaction scenarios.

To learn more about my work, please visit my [website](https://pascal-jansen.github.io/ "website").
